Privacy Policy & Zero-Custody Architecture

Effective Date: November 18, 2026 Entity: Veyroq Protocol & Veyroq Labs

1. Foundational Principle: Client-Side Zero-Custody

Veyroq provides institutional auditability, selective disclosure, and compliance export infrastructure for digital asset treasuries across supported EVM networks.

Non-Custodial Architecture: Veyroq servers never collect, view, store, or custody private spending keys, read-only Scoped View Keys, or decrypted corporate transaction databases. Cryptographic Merkle hashing and local verification execute in client browser memory via the Web Crypto API.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No Spending Keys or View Keys on Backend: Your seed phrases, embedded signer shards, and Scoped View Keys remain local to your wallet signer and client environment.
  • No Financial Plaintext Database: We do not maintain a central database of decrypted transaction values, counterparties, tags, or exported CSV journals.
  • No Custodial Balances: Veyroq is non-custodial software and never holds user funds or stablecoins.

3. Operational, Session & Client-Storage Data Processed

  • Authentication & Signed Session Cookies: Signing in via Privy Email OTP or EVM wallet SIWE issues an HttpOnly HMAC-signed 24-hour cookie (veyroq_session) containing your wallet address, login method, email address (if authenticated with email), and SIWE-verified treasury vault addresses.
  • Local Browser Storage (localStorage): Session metadata (veyroq_auth_session_v1) and custom transaction classification tags remain on your device in localStorage across tab reloads until you sign out, disconnect, or clear browser storage. Volatile decrypted memory state clears when the tab closes.
  • Third-Party Auth, RPC & Explorer APIs: Email login/key management uses Privy (auth.privy.io). Balance, subscription, and USDC transfer lookups query configured RPC nodes (e.g., rpc.testnet.arc.io) and Blockscout explorer APIs (explorer.testnet.arc.io). Backend HTTP logs record request IDs, routes, status codes, and latency with cookies and signatures redacted.

4. Global Privacy Standards (GDPR & CCPA)

By keeping financial records, accounting tags, and View Keys client-side without central financial databases, Veyroq adheres to data-minimization and Privacy-by-Design principles under GDPR and CCPA.

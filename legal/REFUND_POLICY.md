# Veyroq — Institutional Refund & Billing Policy

**Last Updated:** November 18, 2026  
**Effective Date:** November 18, 2026  
**Entity:** Veyroq Protocol & Veyroq Labs (`veyroq.xyz`)  

---

### 1. Scope of Policy
This Refund & Billing Policy applies to all on-chain tier subscriptions, protocol proof anchoring fees, and custom enterprise licensing agreements associated with Veyroq software and services.

---

### 2. Non-Custodial 30-Day On-Chain Passes (No Auto-Renewal)
* Subscriptions purchased through the Veyroq Compliance Console execute directly from your self-custodial wallet (`VeyroqRegistry.sol`) as **manual 30-day term passes** (`months == 1`).
* **Current Arc Testnet Pilot Tiers:** Single Org (**$5 USDC**), Enterprise Treasury (**$50 USDC**), and Audit Firm Partner (**$150 USDC**) per 30-day term.
* **No Auto-Debit / Recurring Charges:** Veyroq never stores payment methods or auto-debits wallets. Access expires automatically after 30 days unless you explicitly sign a new renewal transaction, so no recurring-billing cancellation portal is needed.

---

### 3. On-Chain Protocol & Network Fees (Strictly Non-Refundable)
* All subscription payments, audit proof anchoring fees, and network gas fees paid in USDC are settled directly by immutable smart contracts on-chain.
* **Blockchain transactions are final and irreversible.** Once confirmed on-chain, settled protocol fees cannot be reversed, canceled, or refunded under any circumstances.

---

### 4. Custom Written Enterprise Agreements
Where an enterprise customer executes a separate written annual Master Services Agreement (MSA) with off-chain invoicing, cancellation and refund terms are governed strictly by that executed written agreement.

---

### 5. Billing Disputes
If you believe an erroneous off-chain billing charge occurred, notify our team at `billing@veyroq.xyz` within thirty (30) days of the transaction.

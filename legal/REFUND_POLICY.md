# Veyroq — Institutional Refund & Billing Policy

**Last Updated:** November 1, 2026  
**Effective Date:** November 1, 2026  
**Entity:** Veyroq Protocol & Veyroq Labs (`veyroq.xyz`)  

---

### 1. Scope of Policy
This Refund & Billing Policy applies to all fees, subscriptions, and on-chain protocol settlements associated with Veyroq software and services.

---

### 2. On-Chain Protocol & Network Fees (Strictly Non-Refundable)
* All protocol registration fees, on-chain proof anchoring fees, and network gas fees paid in USDC are processed directly by smart contracts on the host blockchain network.
* **Blockchain transactions are final, deterministic, and irreversible.** 
* Once an audit proof or auditor authorization is committed on-chain, the associated transaction fees cannot be reversed, canceled, or refunded under any circumstances.

---

### 3. Enterprise Subscriptions & Dedicated Services
For customers subscribing to Veyroq Enterprise Services (e.g., dedicated multi-tenant auditor infrastructure, automated enterprise ERP sync, and compliance SLA support):

1. **Monthly Subscriptions:**
   * Monthly subscriptions are billed in advance on a 30-day recurring cycle.
   * You may cancel your subscription at any time via the corporate billing portal. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and no further charges will occur.
   * We do not provide prorated refunds for partial month usage.
2. **Annual Enterprise Agreements:**
   * Enterprise annual agreements may be canceled within seven (7) calendar days of execution for a full refund minus any custom provisioning costs. After 7 days, annual agreements are non-refundable.

---

### 4. Billing Disputes
If you believe a billing error has occurred on your corporate account, notify our billing team at `billing@veyroq.xyz` within thirty (30) days of the transaction.

Refund & Billing Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 Entity: Veyroq Protocol & Veyroq Labs

1. Scope of Policy

This policy governs all software licenses, enterprise tier agreements, and smart contract protocol fees associated with Veyroq.

2. On-Chain Protocol Fees (Non-Refundable)

Deterministic On-Chain Finality: All protocol fees, proof commitment transactions, and network gas fees paid in USDC are broadcast directly to public blockchain networks. Blockchain transactions are immutable and irreversible. Once committed to the smart contract, protocol fees cannot be refunded under any circumstances.

3. Enterprise SaaS & Dedicated Infrastructure Subscriptions

  • Monthly Subscriptions: Billed in advance on a recurring 30-day cycle. You may cancel at any time through the billing dashboard; cancellation takes effect at the conclusion of the active period with no further charges.
  • Annual Enterprise Agreements: May be canceled within seven (7) calendar days of agreement execution for a full refund minus any custom dedicated node provisioning costs incurred.

4. Billing Disputes

If you believe a billing discrepancy has occurred regarding enterprise software licensing, notify our billing team within 30 calendar days at billing@veyroq.xyz.

Refund & Billing Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026 Entity: Veyroq Protocol & Veyroq Labs

1. Scope of Policy

This policy governs all on-chain tier passes, smart contract proof anchoring fees, and any custom enterprise licensing agreements associated with Veyroq.

2. Non-Custodial 30-Day On-Chain Passes (No Auto-Renewal)

  • Manual 30-Day Terms: Subscriptions executed via VeyroqRegistry.sol are fixed 30-day passes (months == 1) signed directly by your wallet. Current Arc Testnet pilot tiers are $5 USDC (Single Org), $50 USDC (Enterprise Treasury), and $150 USDC (Audit Firm Partner).
  • No Auto-Debit: Veyroq never stores credit cards or pulls recurring USDC debits automatically. Passes expire after 30 days unless you explicitly sign a renewal transaction.

3. On-Chain Protocol Fees (Strictly Non-Refundable)

Deterministic On-Chain Finality: All tier pass fees, proof commitment transactions, and network gas fees paid in USDC settle directly on public blockchain networks. Blockchain transactions are immutable and irreversible; once confirmed on-chain, protocol fees cannot be refunded under any circumstances.

4. Custom Written Enterprise Agreements

Separate off-chain invoiced Master Services Agreements (MSA), if executed, are governed by the refund and cancellation terms written into that signed MSA.

5. Billing Disputes

If you believe an erroneous off-chain billing charge has occurred, notify our billing team within 30 calendar days at billing@veyroq.xyz.

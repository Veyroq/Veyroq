Institutional Architecture

The Cryptographic Bridge for Corporate Treasuries

Public ledgers expose trade secrets. Anonymity mixers invite regulatory penalties. Veyroq solves the enterprise dilemma through selective, verifiable disclosure.

PILLAR 01

Scoped View Keys

Provide external auditors read-only cryptographic credentials scoped strictly to a specific quarter or counterparty. View Keys cannot sign transfers or spend treasury funds.

PILLAR 02

Merkle Completeness

Mathematical proof of zero omissions. Auditors verify that every single transaction leaf matches the on-chain root commitment—no secret expenses or hidden revenue.

PILLAR 03

Universal ERP Exports

Instantly maps on-chain cryptographic settlement hashes into standard double-entry journal CSVs pre-configured for QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite, and IRS Form 8949.
